About Me

Data Scientist · Biodiversity · Ecology and Conservation Science

Background

Conservation professional and ecologist with 10+ years bridging data science and climate & conservation strategy across leading NGOs, research institutions, and impact-finance consulting throughout Indonesia. Alongside orangutan and tiger habitat management, marine and terrestrial biodiversity assessment, and spatial planning, a growing part of my work now sits at the intersection of biodiversity and climate action. I also contributed to the drafting of the project design document (PDD) related to carbon, designed forest-restoration programs for carbon sequestration under the Ten Golden Rules for Reforestation, and provided independent verification and monitoring for forest-carbon and biodiversity projects under the VCS Program. I build reproducible data pipelines in R and Python, engage diverse stakeholders from villages to donors, and translate ecological and carbon evidence into decisions that work both on the ground and in the boardroom.

Biodiversity Officer

Jul 2022 – Jun 2026
Fauna & Flora Indonesia Programme
  • Spearheaded ecological management across priority landscapes in West Kalimantan, delivering integrated terrestrial and seascape habitat assessments (HCV–HCS, STAR Metric, PVA).

My Scientific & Open Science Values

Transparency is the bedrock of reproducible science. I advocate for clear workflows - from study design and data acquisition to analytical pipelines and final publication - enabling genuine scrutiny and reuse.

- Jarian Permana

📐 Rigorous & Honest Methods

Selecting the most suitable statistical & ecological approach, quantifying uncertainty, and being explicit about limitations as well as strengths, using reproducible and openly documented workflows in R and Python.

🔬 Evidence into Action

Analysis exists to drive real conservation and investment decisions, not just academic output: METT scoring, PVA, habitat modelling, and MEL frameworks. Sensitive species locations are always protected.

🤝 Grounded in Field & Community

Data grounded in real fieldwork and built through collaboration with local communities, government agencies (BKSDA, DLHK), and donors, because durable conservation is built with people, not just for them.
